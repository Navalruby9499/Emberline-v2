**Established Timeline:** The Long Night and the First Borrowed Dawn

## 1. The Core World & Fundamental Rules

1. **The Great Beacon:**
   - Stands atop the Beacon Summit and historically protected the entire valley from the Long Night.
   - The Beacon is fed by love, purpose, and community bonds, represented by nine hearth shards maintained by nine distinct districts.
   - When the Great Beacon was extinguished, the Long Night fell, and the creeping mist known as **The Hush** swept across the realm.

2. **The Hush & Mopes:**
   - The Hush spreads cold silence and draining mist.
   - Living beings touched by the Hush become **Hushed**—frozen mid-action in grey, stone-like stasis, rather than killed. Restoration is possible when warmth and fire return.
   - **Mopes** are small, erratic fragments of the Hush. Contact extinguishes warmth; striking them disperses their shadow and releases warm **sparks** that restore embers and fill Blaze's skill gauge.

3. **Waystation Beacons:**
   - Ancient roadside lanterns and checkpoints. Lighting a beacon pushes back the local Hush, revives local flora and color, restores Blaze's health, saves player progress, and unlocks an echo memory of Captain Alden's teachings.

4. **The Borrowed Dawn (Version 1 Ending Continuity Lock):**
   - A single hero restoring one hearth shard cannot permanently re-ignite the Great Beacon.
   - Placing Blaze's restored hearth shard at the Summit returns a **borrowed dawn**—a single day of golden warmth, reviving Forge Gate and Captain Alden temporarily.
   - At sunset, without the other eight shards, the Beacon fades and citizens return to stasis. However, Blaze's restored ember remains permanently burning at the Summit as a guidepost for the next hero.
   - The canonical axiom: *"One flame may guide you. Nine flames will bring you home."*

## 2. Protagonist: Blaze (The Crimson Flame)

- **Age:** 19
- **Birthplace:** Forge Gate (the oldest defensive industrial district in Emberline)
- **Role:** Forge Guard (the youngest active member, inducted at 18)
- **Weapon:** **Emberfang** — a dark steel blade forged by her father Rowan, gripped and balanced by her mother Mira, channeled with a pink hearth crystal that ignites with crimson-pink flame.
- **Visual Identity:** Dark tactical combat outfit, silver-pink boots, glowing pink sword, and her signature flowing **crimson scarf**.
- **Family & Mentors:**
  - **Rowan:** Father; master blacksmith who forged Emberfang. Lesson: *Fire can create or destroy.*
  - **Mira:** Mother; retired guard who injured her leg defending travelers in Hollow Hills. Lesson: *Being brave doesn’t mean you never turn back. It means making sure the people who need you still have someone to come home to.*
  - **Captain Alden:** Veteran captain and mentor who named Blaze's flaw (*"You fight like a wildfire... you defeated your opponents and lost the one thing you were supposed to protect"*). Sacrifices himself during the Hush invasion to push Blaze to safety.
- **Character Flaw & Arc:**
  - Blaze starts believing that true bravery means never retreating and overpowering every threat.
  - She carries crushing guilt for Alden's sacrifice, believing she failed him.
  - Across the six zones, she learns restraint, rescue, mercy, timing, and self-acceptance.

## 5. Combat & Controls Specification

- **A / D or Arrow Keys:** Horizontal movement with smooth acceleration, deceleration, and facing flip.
- **Space:** Jump with coyote time (100ms), jump buffering (120ms), and variable jump height based on key hold.
- **J:** **Flame Slash** — Fast 3-hit melee combo with Emberfang producing pink-crimson arcs.
- **K:** **Crescent Wave** — Ranged flaming blade projectile. Consecutive rapid use builds heat; 3 consecutive bursts overheat Emberfang for 2.0s.
- **Shift:** **Ember Dash** — High-speed invulnerable horizontal dash leaving flame trails. 1.2s cooldown.
- **L:** **Crimson Cyclone** — Ultimate screen-clearing spin attack requiring full Skill Meter (charged by collecting sparks and landing slashes).
- **E:** **Interact** — Activate waystation beacons, speak with NPCs, rescue survivors, enter passages.
- **Esc / P:** **Pause Menu** — Sound settings, checkpoint restart, canon codex, return to title.

## 6. Technical Architecture

- **Engine:** Phaser 3/4 runtime with Arcade Physics, integrated seamlessly with React 19 UI shell.
- **Logical Resolution:** 480 × 270 (16:9 2D pixel-art aspect ratio) scaled crisp with nearest-neighbor rendering (`image-rendering: pixelated`).
- **State Management & Persistence:** Versioned client save format (`emberline_save_v1`) stored in `localStorage`, maintaining unlocked zones, collected sparks, checkpoint coordinates, best times, and Turning Point rescue status.
- **Audio Architecture:** Native Web Audio API synthesizer for rich 16-bit chiptune/synth music themes (Forge Gate, Exploration, Boss Battle, Victory, Emotional Dawn) and crisp sound effects (swords, dash, hits, beacon fire).
